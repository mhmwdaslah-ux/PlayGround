GAMEPAD · TOUCH

Gamepads use A pass, B shoot, X through, Y switch, LB tackle, RB sprint. On phone or tablet, use the left stick and the action cluster on the right. Landscape is recommended.

The match pauses at half time. Use the pause menu for tactics, substitutions and settings.

THE RULES, ARCADE STYLE

  • Eleven-a-side, two halves. Goals, throw-ins, corners and goal kicks are live.
  • Offside is implemented and can be toggled in Settings. The HUD always shows it.
  • Fouls can produce free kicks and penalties. Yellow cards are tracked.
  • Three substitutions per match. Stamina drains while sprinting.
  • No online play. Local multiplayer and AI are the complete offering.
TIP: THE ACTIVE PLAYER HAS A GLOWING RING.
